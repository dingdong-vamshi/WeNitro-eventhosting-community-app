Privacy notice

This notice describes the technical data practices implemented by the WeNitro application. The operating company must add its legal name, registered address, grievance contact, and final retention schedule before store submission.

Data we process

Account identity, profile and interests, user-created activities, communities, posts, vibes, messages, selected media, safety reports, consent records, and security metadata needed to provide and protect the service.

Purposes and choices

Core account, safety, and service processing is separated from optional analytics, personalization, and marketing choices. Optional consent can be changed in Settings. We do not place a service-role credential in the application.

Storage and sharing

Application records are protected by PostgreSQL row-level security. Media access is separated into public user-content buckets and a private message bucket. Data is disclosed only to service providers, other users according to the selected audience, or authorities when legally required.

Your controls

Users can request access, correction, erasure, or grievance review in Profile → Settings → Your Data. Users can also control discovery, message requests, distance visibility, follower approval, and optional processing.

Retention and security

Stories expire after 24 hours. Deleted messages and operational sharing records use bounded retention jobs. Requests and safety records are access-controlled and auditable. Exact business retention periods must be approved by counsel before launch.
