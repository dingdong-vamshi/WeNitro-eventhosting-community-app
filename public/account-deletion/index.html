Delete your account and personal data

Account deletion is available to every signed-in user from the mobile or web application.

  1. Sign in to WeNitro.
  2. Open Profile → Settings → Your Data.
  3. Select Delete account and data.
  4. Review and submit the verified erasure request.
Open WeNitro

What happens next

The request is recorded with a due date and can be tracked through Help Chat. Content required for fraud prevention, dispute resolution, security, or legal obligations may be retained only for the applicable period. Other account-linked data is deleted or irreversibly de-identified after verification.
